My Passion for Chess

Chess has been an integral part of my life since childhood. Representing Sri Lanka in multiple international championships has honed my strategic thinking, pattern recognition, and decision-making skills — abilities that directly translate to engineering and research.

I believe chess teaches systematic problem-solving under constraints, a skill essential for robotics and mechatronics. The discipline of analyzing positions, calculating variations, and making trade-offs mirrors the engineering design process.
