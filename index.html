About Me

I am a robotics researcher and engineer interested in the design, modeling, and experimental validation of robotic systems. My work spans robotic mechanisms, wearable sensing, teleoperation, soft robotics, and mathematical modeling.

My approach combines mechanical design and kinematic analysis with simulation, embedded systems, control, and physical prototyping. Across my research projects, I focus on turning mathematical concepts into experimentally validated robotic systems.
