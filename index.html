Chess & Engineering

Chess and engineering share a common foundation: systematic problem-solving under constraints. Just as a chess player evaluates positions, calculates variations, and makes trade-offs, an engineer analyzes systems, models behavior, and optimizes solutions. My chess experience has sharpened my ability to think strategically, anticipate outcomes, and make decisive moves — skills that translate directly to robotics research and teaching.

I am a robotics researcher, educator, and chess player passionate about designing intelligent mechanisms, teaching the next generation of engineers, and applying strategic thinking to complex problems.
